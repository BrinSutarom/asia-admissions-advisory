Questions, answered

Clarity from the start.

When should a student begin?+

Ideally 12–24 months before applications open, especially when profile development is needed. Final-year applicants can still benefit from a focused application sprint.

Do you work only with straight-A students?+

No. Selective admissions are contextual. We begin with an honest profile review, then identify the strongest realistic pathway and where meaningful improvement is still possible.

Do you write essays for students?+

No. The student owns every idea and every final word. We coach structure, reflection, clarity, and revision so the application remains genuinely theirs.

Can you guarantee an offer?+

No responsible advisor can guarantee an admissions decision. We guarantee a rigorous process, candid advice, and an application that represents the student at their strongest.
